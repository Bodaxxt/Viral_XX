1. Commitment to Privacy

Viral-X ("we", "us", or "our") respects the privacy rights of all clients, website visitors, and prospective partners. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning data collection, storage, and processing, designed in compliance with the Egypt Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 151 of 2020) and Qatar's Personal Data Privacy Protection Law (Law No. 13 of 2016).

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal data only when voluntarily provided by you, such as when submitting a project proposal or contacting our team via WhatsApp or email:

  • Contact details: Name, email address, telephone/WhatsApp number, and company name.
  • Project specifics: Budget estimates, timelines, and technical requirements.
  • Technical telemetry: Anonymized IP addresses, browser types, and interaction telemetry used strictly for performance optimization.

3. Customer Intelligence & AI Systems Data Ethics

In our AI Sales Automation and Monthly Customer Intelligence services, all client interaction data is stripped of personally identifiable information (PII) before clustering or analytics generation. We do not sell, rent, or share conversation transcripts with unauthorized third parties or public training sets.
