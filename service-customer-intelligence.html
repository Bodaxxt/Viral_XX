Data Ethics & Realistic Methodology

We do not claim that human emotion or complex sentiment can be inferred with mathematical perfection. All customer intelligence reports are grounded solely in verifiable, consented interaction transcripts with personal information scrubbed in strict compliance with the Egypt Personal Data Protection Law (Law 151) and Qatar’s Personal Data Privacy Protection Law (PDPPL).
