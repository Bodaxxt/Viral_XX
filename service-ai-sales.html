Engineered Reliability

How practical AI sales automation works.

Unlike generic chatbots that make up answers, Viral-X AI sales agents operate under strict deterministic guardrails.

⚗

Strict Catalog Grounding

The agent's knowledge boundary is strictly locked to your verified database of products, inventory, prices, and shipping policies. If an item isn't in stock, the agent never pretends it is.

☑

Safe Draft Cart & Gate

When a customer indicates purchase intent, the agent compiles an itemized draft reservation or cart. It requires explicit user confirmation before any transaction is passed to the checkout layer.

♀

Instant Human Escalation

Bespoke jewelry sizing, high-value corporate inquiries, or complex custom requests are instantly flagged and transferred to a human specialist via WhatsApp with complete conversational context.
