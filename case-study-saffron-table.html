Concept Project Cairo & Doha Self-Initiated Concept

The Saffron Table: An editorial culinary digital experience.

Conceived for an upscale contemporary restaurant bridging Cairo’s culinary innovation with Doha’s fine dining patronage. Designed around warm atmospheric photography, interactive chef tasting menus, and effortless table reservation holds.

Sector
Hospitality & Fine Dining
Location Context
Zamalek (Cairo) & Katara (Doha)
Design Direction
Warm Amber, Charcoal, Saffron
Core Flow
Multi-Seating Tasting Hold
01 / The Fictional Challenge

Conveying culinary artistry and securing reservations without PDF menus.

Too many fine dining websites in the region suffer from clunky third-party booking widgets, unreadable PDF menus that fail on mobile phones, and clinical photography that fails to convey the scent, warmth, and ambience of the dining room.

The challenge was to design a sensory, editorial web presence where visitors can explore the chef’s seasonal tasting menus, understand spice provenance (such as Iranian saffron and Sinai sumac), filter dishes by dietary preference, and reserve a table within three seamless taps.

02 / Intended Audience

Gastronomy enthusiasts, executive diners, and celebratory guests.

Diners seeking memorable gastronomic experiences in Cairo and Doha: corporate leaders hosting private client dinners, couples celebrating anniversaries, and visiting culinary travelers. They require absolute clarity regarding dress code, cancellation policies, valet parking, and allergen accommodations.

03 / Design Direction

Sensory warmth, rich typography, and cinematic lighting.

The aesthetic draws inspiration from high-end culinary monographs:

Deep Charcoal (#1B181E)
Dark dining room ambiance that makes food photography pop.
Saffron Ochre (#D97706)
Warm spice accent evoking culinary intimacy and glow.
Parchment Cream (#FAF7F2)
Tactile editorial paper background for daytime menu reading.
04 / Key Interface Decisions

Streamlined culinary interaction.

1. Live Tasting Menu Explorer (No Clunky PDFs)

Menus are rendered as pure, accessible web typography with instant toggle filters for Vegetarian, Pescatarian, and Halal-certified options. Each dish reveals ingredient origins upon hover or tap.

2. Time-Slot Reservation Hold

A clean reservation selector that displays exact seating availability for 19:30 and 21:30 seatings, automatically calculates group deposit policies, and triggers instant SMS/WhatsApp confirmation receipts.

3. Private Dining Room (PDR) Concierge Flow

For parties of 8 to 20 guests, an interactive inquiry builder lets hosts specify personalized bespoke menus, floral arrangements, and audio/visual requirements.

05 / Technical Architecture

High-speed reservation engine and media optimization.

A lightweight front-end connected to SevenRooms or OpenTable API webhooks, backed by edge caching for rapid menu updates. All culinary imagery is optimized with progressive image decoding so that pages render instantly on mobile networks.
