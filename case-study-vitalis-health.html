Concept Project New Cairo & Regional Self-Initiated Concept

Vitalis Clinical Health: A calm, trustworthy healthcare portal.

Conceived for an advanced outpatient wellness and executive medical clinic in New Cairo. Designed to eliminate clinical anxiety, present verified physician credentials, clarify screening preparation, and make booking consultation appointments effortless.

Sector
Healthcare & Executive Wellness
Location Context
North 90th St, New Cairo
Design Direction
Warm Sand, Oak, Clinical Emerald
Core Flow
Specialist Screening Pathways
01 / The Fictional Challenge

Transforming medical portals from intimidating directories into reassuring journeys.

Patients seeking specialized diagnostic care often encounter cluttered clinic websites with endless doctor lists, opaque pricing, ambiguous preparation instructions (such as fasting rules), and confusing booking forms that lead to phone tag.

The challenge was to design a serene, architectural healthcare interface that instills immediate confidence, outlines comprehensive executive wellness screening packages transparently, and provides 1-tap navigation to the physical clinic suites in New Cairo.

02 / Intended Audience

Patients prioritizing proactive longevity, executive screening, and family medicine.

Corporate executives, expatriates, and regional families in Egypt seeking international-standard outpatient diagnostics. They require unambiguous credentials (e.g. Royal College certifications, European Board certifications), strict patient privacy assurances, and frictionless scheduling.

03 / Design Direction

Serene architectural minimalism: Warm oak, soft stone, and restorative tones.

Rather than generic sterile medical blues, we established a tranquil aesthetic reminiscent of a Scandinavian wellness retreat:

Warm Stone (#F8F7F4)
Soft natural backdrop that reduces eye strain and medical stress.
Clinical Emerald (#059669)
Symbolizing health vitality, verified appointments, and care.
Slate Oak (#2D2738)
Authoritative structural contrast for physician credentials.
04 / Key Interface Decisions

Reassuring, accessible patient pathways.

1. Guided Screening Package Flow

Instead of searching medical jargon, patients select their focus (Cardiovascular Longevity, Metabolic Health, Executive Annual Panel) and instantly view what is included, preparation instructions, and transparent package fees.

2. Verified Physician Profiles with Direct Booking

Each physician profile showcases verified fellowships, languages spoken (English, Arabic, French), research interests, and real-time open consultation slots.

3. 1-Tap Clinic Navigation & Parking Instructions

Clear directions for visitors driving down North 90th Street in New Cairo, with explicit details on underground client parking, elevator accessibility, and wheelchair routes.

05 / Technical Architecture

Compliant, accessible, and privacy-shielded EHR integration.

Compliant with Egypt’s Personal Data Protection Law (Law 151) and HIPAA/GDPR health data principles. Patient appointment bookings are routed through encrypted webhook conduits directly into the clinic's local Electronic Health Record (EHR) system with zero storage of sensitive health identifiers on unverified public cloud servers.
